Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how Vezir (“we”, “the service”), an AI assistant operated by Elsen Ibrahimov in Baku, Azerbaijan, handles personal data. Vezir is currently in a private pilot with a limited number of invited users.

1. Data we process

  • Account data: your name, phone number or messenger user ID, preferred language.
  • Conversation data: messages you send to Vezir and Vezir’s replies, including content you ask Vezir to remember (for example people, preferences, dates and decisions).
  • Action data: drafts of messages or actions Vezir proposes, and your approval or rejection of them.
  • Technical data: timestamps, message IDs and usage statistics needed to operate and bill the service.

2. Why we process it

  • To provide the assistant: answer your questions, remember context you share and prepare the actions you ask for.
  • To ask for your approval before anything is sent to other people on your behalf.
  • To keep the service secure, measure costs and fix problems.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Service providers

To deliver the service we use the following processors, which receive only the data needed for their function:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) and Telegram — to receive and deliver messages.
  • Anthropic (Claude) and other AI model providers — to generate responses. Under their API terms, content sent through the API is not used to train their models.
  • Cloud hosting providers — to store data and run the service.

4. Retention

Conversation and memory data are kept for as long as your account is active, so the assistant can remember context. You can ask Vezir to forget a specific fact at any time. When you close your account, your data is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion, and you can withdraw consent at any time. To do so, write to vezir.ai.contact@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

6. Data deletion

To delete all your data, send a message “delete my data” to Vezir or email vezir.ai.contact@gmail.com from the phone number or account you use with Vezir. We will confirm when deletion is complete.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, access is limited to the operator, and each business’s data is kept separate from others.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and note the date above.
